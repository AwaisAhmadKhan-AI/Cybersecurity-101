# BitLocker

## Overview

**BitLocker** is a Windows feature that **encrypts your drive** to protect your data.

If your computer is **lost or stolen**, BitLocker prevents unauthorized people from accessing your files.

---

# How BitLocker Works

BitLocker encrypts (scrambles) all the data on your drive.

- ✅ Authorized users can access the data normally.
- ❌ Anyone without the correct authentication cannot read the data.

This helps protect sensitive information from theft.

---

# BitLocker and TPM

BitLocker provides the **best protection** when used with a **Trusted Platform Module (TPM)**.

**TPM (Trusted Platform Module)** is a hardware security chip that:

- Securely stores encryption keys.
- Helps verify that the computer has not been tampered with.
- Works with BitLocker to unlock the drive safely.

---

# Benefits of BitLocker

- Protects data if a device is lost or stolen.
- Encrypts the entire drive.
- Prevents unauthorized access to files.
- Works with TPM for stronger security.

# Key Points

- **BitLocker** is Windows' full-disk encryption feature.
- It protects data from unauthorized access.
- It is most secure when used with a **TPM** chip.
- TPM securely stores encryption keys and helps detect system tampering.
- BitLocker is especially useful for laptops and portable devices.
